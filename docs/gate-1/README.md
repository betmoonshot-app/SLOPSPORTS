**Status:** Complete, awaiting review.

## What this is

Gate 1 of a four-gate plan. The purpose is to prove **on paper** that the show works before spending money, time, or production cycles on it.

Nothing here is approved for production. The point of a gate is that it can fail.

## The cast, resolved

- **CHALK** — knows every number, understands nothing about why anyone cares
- **DOG** — has absorbed every argument fans have ever made and believes all of it
- **BOOTH** — the producer keeping a show on the air with two hosts broken in opposite directions

The debate is structurally generated: CHALK and DOG see genuinely different evidence, so they disagree without performing. BOOTH is the audience surrogate and the emotional anchor — the role the precedent research identified as the strongest predictor of long-term attachment to an AI character.

---

## Gate 1 pass criteria

**Read the pilot script out loud, cold, with three human voices.**

It passes if:

1. It's entertaining as a piece of sports content **independent of the AI angle**
2. "Help Me Understand" lands as moving rather than twee
3. BOOTH earns its slot rather than reading as a fifth wheel
4. The ledger reads as a hook rather than as bookkeeping
5. **The football is good enough to hold a real NFL fan**

If #5 fails, nothing else matters.

## What Gate 1 surfaced that changed the plan

Three findings worth flagging:

1. **TTS costs ~$99/month, not ~$22.** A 4–5× correction. Real production regenerates lines at roughly 2.5–3× the final character count.
2. **The $20K/month Month-12 target is not reachable** from this show alone in Year 1. Recommended replacement planning number: ~$2,000/month net. Break-even (~$237/mo) is genuinely modest and reachable.
3. **Sportsbook affiliate revenue is excluded entirely** pending legal review — and it was the largest revenue line in Business Plan v4.0. That decision is still open.

## Gates 1.5–4

**Gate 1.5 — Written Format Validation.** The site launches first. Character system prompts, 10 finished posts across five columns, editorial standards, site build, publishing pipeline. Pass condition: you'd read a second post from each character, and end-to-end production takes under 40 minutes. Full detail in doc 05.

**Gate 2 — Production Proof.** Voice auditions first (the protocol is in doc 03 — run it before anything else). Then the fully produced pilot, a production workflow document, and a legal risk memo. Pass condition: you listen end-to-end and think *"I'd listen to Episode 2"* — not *"that's impressive for AI."*

**Gate 3 — Launch Readiness.** Brand kit, six episodes in the can, distribution setup, 30-day launch plan.

**Gate 4 — 90-Day Review.** Decision gate against the kill criteria in the show bible.
